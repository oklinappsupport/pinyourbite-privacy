PinYourBite Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

PinYourBite is a personal restaurant journal. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles information when you use it.

Information you create and store

PinYourBite stores the restaurant information and reviews that you choose to enter, including restaurant names, addresses, map locations, ratings, notes, tags, cost information, ordered items, and photos. This information is stored locally on your device so that the app can display your restaurant journal, search results, rankings, map pins, and backups.

Location information

If you grant location permission, PinYourBite uses your device location while the app is in use to center the map, help you add nearby places, and calculate distance filters. PinYourBite does not request background location access.

Maps, address lookup, and map links

When you search for an address or paste a Google Maps link, the app may send the address or link to mapping, geocoding, or Google Maps services to retrieve place information. When maps are displayed, the map provider may receive technical information such as your IP address and map-area requests as part of delivering map tiles.

Advertising

PinYourBite uses Google AdMob to display ads. Google Mobile Ads may collect and process information such as device and account identifiers, IP address, app interaction information, and diagnostic information for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and related purposes. Google handles this information under its own policies, including the Google Privacy Policy. Where required by law, the app will present applicable advertising privacy choices.

Sharing of information

PinYourBite does not operate an account system or a developer-controlled cloud database for your restaurant journal. The app does not sell your restaurant entries or reviews. Information is shared only as described above: with services needed for optional map/address lookups and with Google AdMob for advertising.

Backups and exports

You may choose to export a backup from the app. Exported files are saved to the location you select and are under your control. If you share an exported file, you are responsible for the information it contains. Android device backup features may also back up app data when enabled by you or your device settings.

Retention and deletion

Your restaurant journal remains on your device until you delete individual restaurants or visits, clear app data, or uninstall the app. You can delete exported backup files from the location where you saved them. PinYourBite has no user accounts and does not retain a server-side copy of your restaurant journal.

Security

Restaurant journal data is stored in the app's private device storage. Network communications with supported services are handled using their standard secure transport mechanisms. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please use care when entering or exporting personal information.

Children's privacy

PinYourBite is not directed to children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us so we can assist.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when PinYourBite's features or data practices change. The current version will be posted at this URL with an updated effective date.
